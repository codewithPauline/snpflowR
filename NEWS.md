# snpflowR 0.0.0.9000

Initial research development version. No stable release or CRAN submission.

* Add validated allele-count and genotype constructors and audited locus QC.
* Add restricted diploid biallelic SNP VCF input, with sample-name alignment.
* Implement the published migrant-pool construction with plug-in multilocus GST.
* Expose no-shared-allele exclusions and singular descriptive migration transforms.
* Add paired locus/block contrast bootstrap, unresolved labels, and interval plots.
* Add a seeded forward neutral simulator, automated tests, and research protocols.
